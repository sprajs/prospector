First close the precise source and numerical dependencies of the conventional
comparison below. In parallel, reserve ordinary literature work for an unusual
mechanism or a challenge to an existing idea. Neither native full-CMB development
nor all nineteen historical source scopes must finish before a supported external

Choose one deliverable, named sources and explicit stopping criteria from this
plan. Read saved receipts before fetching. For ordinary readings, use actual

requested versus exposed backend identity. Targeted source checks retain their
separate stage and unread scope. This reset's user-requested Astra consultation

A batch remains bounded by AGENTS.md: at most 20 admitted versions, six full

quotas. Queue-before-discovery still applies. Never-searched lanes are discovery
coverage information, not scientific priority or proof that their literature is
absent. Coordinate at most four shared compiler/compute jobs; this planning review
uses zero. A reading priority authorizes no experiment, schedule or Site deployment.

## Conventional reference and next accepted experiment design

The working conventional model is the complete **source-defined flat GR base
ΛCDM** of Planck 2018 VI `1807.06209v4`: baryons, CDM, photons, two effectively
massless neutrinos, one fixed 0.06 eV neutrino, Λ, adiabatic power-law scalar
initial perturbations, BBN-consistent helium, recombination and declared tanh
reionization. The pinned reference fixes Tcmb=2.7255 K, Neff=3.046 and primordial
pivot k=0.05 Mpc^-1; its helium relation uses the source PArthENoPE grid and
neutron-lifetime convention. A modern BBN/recombination prescription earns its
own explicitly mapped comparison identity. Source Table 2 marginal summaries are contextual posterior values,
not independent measurements, a complete joint covariance or an exact draw.

The next comparison should vary all six source coordinates, with the chosen
parameterization declared: physical baryon/CDM densities, source acoustic-scale
coordinate (or an explicitly mapped H0 coordinate), optical depth, scalar amplitude
and scalar tilt. θMC is a sampling proxy; it cannot silently become exact θ*.
Record coordinate Jacobians when comparing priors. Source sampling conventions
and a CLASS-compatible parameterization must be reconciled rather than inferred
from the table of marginal means.

Prospector's first deliverable is a finite baseline contract specifying:

- Friedmann/continuity and perturbation equations, gauge/initial-mode conventions,
  neutrino mass/count/temperature conventions, helium/BBN and recombination owner,
  reionization history, units, input maps and derived closure.
- Exact likelihood components, released inputs, calibration/nuisance treatment,
  priors/support and the posterior or profile objective. Existing official primary
  PLC components can be selected explicitly; Planck's Table 2
  `TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing` identity additionally requires its reconstruction likelihood and baseline Plik conditioning. Existing
  Commander/SimAll/Plik-lite scores are a distinct primary-CMB target; do not call
  their product Table 2 parity. Fixed YHe is a different identity from a BBN relation.
- Data ancestry and overlaps, including which BAO/SN/calibrator measurements are
  reused. A product of independently named probes earns no independence by name.
  Source-justified negligible dependence plus a declared sensitivity analysis can
  support a bounded target; an exact universal cross-covariance is not required.
- Exact CLASS/likelihood revisions, input hashes, candidate version and the
  numerical checks required before fitting. Leave unverified prior bounds and
  covariance null with reasons; do not guess them from published uncertainties.

Reproducible must qualify numerical precision over the admitted reference-fit
domain. The observed +0.2996146 shift in separate Planck log likelihood at the
w=-0.9,H0=75 test exceeds its 0.2 budget: keep that alternative-domain refusal
and close it before including that region in an expanded comparison. It does
not automatically veto a separately qualified ΛCDM-only domain. Optimizer-bracket refinement
is a separate diagnostic. Existing official test-vector success and identical
fresh runs do not settle solver/table/interpolation sensitivity. Native full-CMB
physics remains a parallel Irreducible programme and is not this external route's
prerequisite.

Keep the synthetic massless supplied-zdrag comparison and late radiation-free
`background.evaluate` identity separate from this baseline. Derive matter from
its declared baryon/CDM state when removing massive neutrinos; preserve retained
and lost scope. Supplied drag is not predicted drag; ideal acoustic/collisionless
controls do not supply recombination, transfer functions or an observed CMB
likelihood. The chosen w=-0.9 PPF conditional result remains negative evidence for
that fixed-state branch, not a generic exclusion of constant-w cosmology.

## Combination hypotheses and rejection tests

Combination proposals below are new planning inferences, not author claims,
accepted graph edges or modified register records. A negative compatibility result
is useful. A combination must outperform its components as an explanatory test,
not merely provide more adjustable parameters.

| Hypothesis | Why it could be informative | Cheapest test and rejection condition |
|---|---|---|
| A transient early component plus a late scalar shoulder | Separates ruler reduction from late acceleration and tests whether one field versus two independent sectors makes distinct timing/growth predictions. | Write one action/continuity system and baseline sector map. Reject double-counted Λ/vacuum, incompatible initial conditions or missing energy budget; require a perturbation/growth discriminator after background degeneracy. |
| A triggered thermal sector plus decaying-parent daughters | Links onset/late transfer through a specified physical trigger rather than arbitrary transition times. | Close trigger/radial/recoil/dark-radiation energy and thermal history; reject overlapping species counts or absent production law. Compare a predicted transition/BBN/phase signature, without assuming NEDE and staged decay are the same field. |
| Conserved GR geometry plus halo/DF/lens physics | Connects distances, deflections and kinematics to one explicit gravity and tracer contract. | Check D_l,D_s,D_ls, SI mass/radius, critical-density convention, physical/reduced deflection and DF support. Reject a substituted tracer or unsupported observational weighting; a composite NFW/Hernquist potential requires a new tracer DF/Jeans law rather than reusing the self-gravitating Plummer DF. |
| Matched background plus alternate perturbation closure | Makes the background-equivalence fallacy experimentally testable for EDE/Chaplygin/DGP branches. | Derive degrees of freedom and gauge/initial-mode maps, then a single phase/growth/shear prediction with shared input lineage. Reject inferred equivalence from H(a) alone. |
| A clock/averaging model plus calibrated candle/lens data | Tests whether a putative geometry signal survives source-dependent reduction and observer definitions. | Declare emission/observation clocks and redshift/flux operators on exact common objects. Reject implicit FLRW recalibration or unknown joint dependence; incompatible metrics are competing branches, not additive corrections. |

Before promoting any combination, review gravity/geometry, action or conservation,
parameter/initial-condition compatibility, background and perturbation closure,
observable operator, domain/stability and shared observation ancestry. Keep uncertain
relationships provisional and use `informs` for criticism/constraints rather than
making them physical components. No union of topics proves compatibility.
