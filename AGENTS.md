# Working in Prospector

Prospector discovers and reviews cosmology literature and prepares traceable
candidate investigations. Reproducible owns experiment designs, numerical runs
and plots. Irreducible owns shared physical models and scientific computation.
Read the current README and inspect actual source/records before claiming that
an acquisition route, workflow, schema or validator is implemented.

Preserve exact paper/version identities, original-source locators, source hashes,
reading coverage, review provenance, corrections and unresolved limitations.
Distinguish observations, fitted results, authors' proposals and your own inference.
Shared data are not independent evidence. A candidate may remain blocked; do not
replace missing physics with an unrelated convenient calculation.

Keep downloaded papers and full text in ignored local storage. Publish short
original notes and descriptive provenance after checking terms. Coordinate shared
indices and preserve other workers' uncommitted work. Follow the detailed reading
and scientific-review instructions when those are present in this checkout.

For requested repository changes, work on a `codex/` branch, commit coherent
checkpoints, push them promptly and open a reviewable PR. Follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [development](docs/development.md).
Independent review and applicable green CI on the latest head/base precede an
authorized merge. Direct pushes to `main` and force pushes require a specific
request. A literature batch does not authorize numerical experiments, scheduling
or external messages. Preserve dirty/active worktrees and extra commits during
verified post-merge cleanup; never force-delete branches.
