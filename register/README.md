## Source corrections

[2026-10-01-initial-science: 17 recorded corrections](reviews/2026-10-01-initial-science.json)

- **arxiv:1811.04083v2**: The printed angular-scale ratio contradicts its defining equation. The review preserves the source and records the reciprocal algebraic repair separately.
- **arxiv:1910.10739v5**: NEDE uses a before-minus-after jump convention; omitting it reverses the apparent sign of the pressure-induced H-dot change.
- **arxiv:1009.5855v2**: The historical MLCS samples do not all favour timescape. Overlapping samples, reductions, priors and cuts must stay separate.
- **arxiv:1009.5855v2**: The extracted clock-rate example mixes percent and fraction notation; it is a model illustration, not a new measurement.

[2026-10-01-late-expansion-science: 15 recorded corrections](reviews/2026-10-01-late-expansion-science.json)

- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Source coupling/action/effective-potential conventions conflict; do not compile them verbatim.
- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Acoustic angle needs matched comoving or physical ruler-distance conventions.
- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Restore source absolute-value bars in the plateau distance estimate.
- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Double-exponential slope equations have reversed signs; Eq. (5.13) supplies the consistent identity.
- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Table 1 vacuum-like point A is exact only at zero slope.
- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Baryon correction sign conflicts with prose; quoted 3.75% floor is asymptotic.
- **arxiv:2605.26116v2**: Acceleration depends on total EOS, not scalar EOS alone.
- **arxiv:2602.01543v2**: SIDM effective cubic coupling is dimensionful; source omits its unit.
- **arxiv:2602.01543v2**: Resolve individual versus relative velocity before using SIDM curves.
- **arxiv:2602.01543v2**: Approximate background match cannot transfer fitted likelihood evidence.
- **arxiv:2609.34239v1**: Luna corrected the figure-coverage conflict with a fresh page-12 check; coverage history is preserved.
- **arxiv:2609.34239v1**: n+1 nodes yield n free coefficients because x_n is fixed.
- **arxiv:2609.34239v1**: delta q=0 above cutoff does not by itself normalize H to LambdaCDM.
- **arxiv:2609.34239v1**: Selected fitted q upturn is not a measured jerk or acceleration endpoint.
- **arxiv:2609.34239v1**: Planck distance priors are conditional inferred summaries, not full CMB validation.
